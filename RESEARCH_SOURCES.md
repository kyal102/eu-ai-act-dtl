## Source-use rules

1. The Regulation is the legal source; Commission guidance and Service Desk
   summaries are implementation aids and are not themselves binding law.
2. A source date is part of the evidence state. Recheck this file when the Act,
   Digital Omnibus amendments, guidance, code of practice or timeline changes.
3. A DTL PASS is evidence that a declared technical check passed. It is not a
   conclusion that the legal obligation is satisfied.
